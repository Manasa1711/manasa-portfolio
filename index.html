Smart Precipitation Classification and Forecasting System

A Streamlit app that predicts whether it will rain, snow or stay clear, gives a risk alert, forecasts humidity for the next 3 days, and answers plain-English weather questions.

  • Cleaned 96,000+ hourly weather records with Pandas: missing values, duplicates and scaling
  • Created features such as a temperature difference from a rolling average and a humidity index
  • Trained and compared 4 classification models; Decision Tree scored highest
  • Risk alerts: critical when rain confidence is above 90%
  • 3-day humidity forecast with an ARIMA time-series model
  • Natural-language questions understood by a Hugging Face zero-shot model (DeBERTa)
